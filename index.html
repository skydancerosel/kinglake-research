Services

What we do

AI and machine learning research

Research-grade analysis of how models learn and what they compute.

  • Mechanistic interpretability and circuit analysis
  • Training dynamics and diagnostics
  • Experiment design, evaluation, and reproducible pipelines

Formal verification

Machine-checked mathematics with evidence that it says what it should.

  • Formalization in Lean 4 and Mathlib
  • Faithfulness audits: does the formal statement match the source?
  • Human-readable verification reports for non-specialists

Quantitative research

Systematic trading research with machine learning at its core.

  • Neural-network signals for market-neutral long/short strategies
  • Portfolio construction, backtesting, and robustness testing
  • Risk and regime analysis

AI workflows and software

We also design and build working systems for clients.

  • AI-assisted workflows with built-in verification and review
  • Data pipelines and internal tools
  • Web and mobile applications
